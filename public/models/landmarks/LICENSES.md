# Landmark model provenance

`hq-tower.glb`, `civic-hub.glb`, and `transit-hub.glb` are derived from
Kenney's City Kit (Commercial), distributed under CC0 1.0.

Source pack: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial

The files were obtained as self-contained GLB conversions from the
Hidencod/tge-assets CC0 model library. They are bundled locally and are never
requested from a third-party service by the running game.
